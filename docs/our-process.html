Our Installation Process

Step-by-Step Permanent Lighting Installation

Step 1: Request Your Quote

Start by sending us your home address through our online form. We use Google Street View to provide a preliminary estimate for your permanent lighting installation without requiring an in-person visit. This gives you a quick, low-pressure way to understand the investment for your permanent Christmas lights system.

If you'd like a more accurate estimate, we're happy to schedule an in-person consultation where we can take precise measurements and discuss your specific vision for your permanent lighting installation.

Step 2: Design & Planning

Once you've decided to move forward, we work with you to design your permanent lighting system. We'll discuss your home's architecture, your lighting preferences, and any specific requirements. Our team ensures the design complements your home while maximizing the visual impact of your permanent lighting installation.

We'll provide a detailed plan showing track placement, color matching options, and system specifications. This ensures we're aligned on the vision before installation begins.

Step 3: Professional Installation

Our insured, professional installers arrive on time and ready to work. We begin by carefully mounting the powder-coated aluminum tracks along your roofline, ensuring they're color-matched to your home's trim for minimal visibility during the day.

Every connection is sealed for waterproof protection, and every component is installed to our exacting standards. Our professional installation process ensures your permanent light installation is built to last, with attention to detail that sets Pixel Peak apart.

Step 4: System Setup & Testing

After installation, we configure your permanent lighting system and connect it to your smartphone app. We test every component to ensure flawless operation, from individual LED modules to the complete system.

We'll walk you through the app controls, show you how to create custom color schemes, and demonstrate the various lighting modes available. Your permanent Christmas lights system is ready to use immediately after installation.

Step 5: Final Walkthrough & Satisfaction

We complete every permanent lighting installation with a final walkthrough to ensure your complete satisfaction. We'll review the installation, test the system together, and answer any questions you may have about your new permanent lighting system.

Our commitment to customer satisfaction means we won't consider the job complete until you're happy with every aspect of your permanent light installation. As a local company, we're here for you long after installation is complete, ready to assist with any questions or support you may need.
